01 · Research

Methods for complex biomedical data

Dr. Chen develops statistical and machine-learning methods for complex, dependent biomedical data, with an emphasis on reliable individual-level inference, cross-study reproducibility, and trustworthy AI/ML.

Structured dependence

Modeling networks, high-throughput features, and spatiotemporal structure across neuroimaging and multi-omics data.

Biomedical inference

Methodology and collaboration spanning real-world evidence, clinical trials, genetics, cancer, and population health.
